# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with Tailwind CSS v4, built as static files. npm and Prettier (with the Astro plugin). Netlify builds the repo; the owner controls the domain and the deploy.

## Users

- Recruiters and hiring managers who check a candidate in a few minutes, often on a phone, and compare the site with LinkedIn.
- Possible freelance clients who want proof that Victor can ship a real product.
- Peers and people who know Victor and want to see what he builds.

## Product Purpose

viaxco.com is the personal site of Victor Ajibade, a software engineer in Abuja, Nigeria, who works remotely. It is his home on the web. It shows what he builds and how he thinks, and it gives a direct way to contact him. Success: a visitor leaves with a clear picture of the systems Victor built and a reason to email him.

## Positioning

Most of Victor's best work is private company code. The site shows the systems he built, not screenshots of team products: one codebase that serves three country sites, a shared UI library, a long platform upgrade, and a sign-in flow across domains. The claim is specific and honest about who did what.

## Operating Context

- One static page. No blog, no CV link, no testimonials in this release.
- The owner edits the copy in the repo after the first draft.
- Content order: intro, Kudy work, contract work, side projects, client sites, earlier work (2021), about, contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current role: Frontend Engineer at Kudy Financials (kudy.io, kudy.ng, kudy.us), June 2023 to now. The site title is "Software engineer". Do not use "Senior".
- Kudy code, internal URLs, customer data, business numbers, PR or commit counts and colleague quotes must not appear.
- Use "led" or "built" only where Victor did most of the work: the multi-region platform, the shared UI library, the TypeScript/Vite/Astro/React 19 upgrades, the cross-region sign-in. Use "worked on" or "with the team" for product features and page design. A Kudy designer drew the pages.
- No screenshots of Kudy, Turn Up or Eko. Turn Up and Eko are text only.
- Numbers appear only when the number is the story (3 sites, 0% to 100% of source files in TypeScript).
- Contact: ajibadevic@gmail.com, GitHub ViaxCo, LinkedIn victor-ajibade. No X.
- "Open to select freelance work" is shown. "Open to roles" is not.

## Brand Commitments

- The name "Victor Ajibade" leads. "ViaxCo" is the handle and the domain.
- Do not call this site a version (no "v4", "v5").
- Dark theme only. No light theme and no theme toggle.
- Visual register: the category-standard engineer portfolio, played straight at full craft. The craft bar is brittanychiang.com, paco.me and leerob.com. No themed or costume worlds.
- Mobile-first.

## Product Principles

1. Show the system, not the screen.
2. Be exact about credit. Trust comes from accuracy.
3. The work leads; the chrome recedes.
4. Every claim must survive a check against git, LinkedIn or a live URL.
